# Md Tayve Hossin Jony - Personal Portfolio 🔬⚡

Welcome to the personal portfolio website of **Md Tayve Hossin Jony**, an undergraduate researcher in **Materials Science and Engineering (MSE)** at **Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)**.

## 🌟 Focus Areas
- **Computational Materials Science:** Density Functional Theory (DFT) with Quantum ESPRESSO 7.5, structure relaxation, band structure calculations, and CI-NEB Na⁺ migration barriers.
- **Next-Generation Energy Storage:** Biomass-derived nitrogen-doped porous carbon (NPC) and 2D Ti₃C₂Tₓ MXene anodes for flexible sodium-ion batteries.
- **Machine Learning in Materials:** Graph Neural Networks (GNN) and Bayesian optimization for closed-loop defect discovery.
- **Scientific Visualization:** Python, Streamlit, Plotly, HTML5 Canvas, VESTA, and OVITO.
